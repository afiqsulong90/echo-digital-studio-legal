Summary

Color Tube Sort is a puzzle game that runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There is no account, no login, and no server that receives your data. Your game progress is saved locally on your device only.

Data stored on your device

The app saves exactly one thing: the number of the level you have reached, stored in your device's local app storage as a single integer. Nothing else about your play is recorded — no name, no scores history, no play times, no device details.

This value is kept entirely on your device. It is never sent anywhere, because the app has no internet permission and no networking code. You can clear local progress via your device's app settings. Android backup and restore, if enabled, may preserve app settings.

Internet access

This version of the app does not require an internet connection to play and does not send any information over the network. If a future version adds optional advertising or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the update will be described in the app's release notes.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not collect any data. This version of the app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is no data to access, correct, export, or delete on our side. All game data lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. If you have any question about this policy, contact us at the address above.

Security

Since no data is transmitted or stored off-device, there is no server-side data to secure. Local game data is protected by your device's own operating system security.

Changes to this policy
